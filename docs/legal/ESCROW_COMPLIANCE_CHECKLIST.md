# ExpoCraft Escrow Compliance Checklist

Энэ draft нь product/legal review-д зориулсан checklist. Монгол дахь төлбөр зуучлал, бусдын мөнгө түр хадгалах, payout хийх эрх зүйн эцсийн зөвлөгөөг лицензтэй хуульч/санхүүгийн зөвлөхөөр баталгаажуулна.

## Required Policies

- Buyer Terms of Service
- Seller Agreement
- Escrow and settlement policy
- Refund and dispute policy
- Privacy policy
- Prohibited goods and handmade authenticity policy
- Payout/KYC information collection policy

## Escrow Flow To Review

1. Buyer pays one cart payment.
2. Platform records escrow hold in ledger.
3. Order splits by seller.
4. Buyer confirms receipt or auto-release window expires.
5. Platform deducts commission.
6. Seller available balance becomes payout eligible.
7. Dispute freezes escrow until admin resolution.

## Legal Questions

- Does ExpoCraft need a payment/e-money/payment facilitator license?
- Can buyer funds be held in the platform operating account, or is a segregated account required?
- What disclosure must be shown before checkout?
- What is the maximum settlement delay?
- What seller KYC is required before payout?
- How are refunds, chargebacks, and partial disputes handled?
- What tax/e-barimt obligation applies for commission and seller sales?
- What records must be retained, and for how long?

## Launch Gate

Do not enable live escrow/payment at public scale until:

- Legal sign-off is recorded.
- Terms are published in Mongolian and English.
- Seller agreement is accepted during onboarding.
- Checkout shows escrow/refund disclosure.
- Admin can export audit and ledger records.
- Reconciliation is tested against provider settlement reports.
